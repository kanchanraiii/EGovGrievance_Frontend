Platform Overview

  • Five roles (Admin, Supervisory Officer, Department Officer, Case Worker, Citizen) with JWT-based role-driven access control.
  • Seven-stage grievance lifecycle: submitted → assigned → dept_review → in_progress → resolved → closed → escalated.
  • Department Officers and Case Workers manage grievance investigation and resolution.
  • Supervisory Officers monitor deadlines and handle escalations, while Admins manage departments and users.
  • Citizens submit and track grievances, with automated CI/CD deployment via Jenkins pipeline.
